How it plays

  1. Tap the matchstick that's in the wrong place — it lifts and glows.
  2. Drop it in the empty slot to make the equation true.
  3. Score points for the difficulty, the time you had left, and your streak.

What's inside

  • 500 puzzles across five difficulties, Easy through Master
  • A new Daily Challenge every day, with a streak bonus
  • Hints when you're stuck, and a scratch pad to work it out on
  • Badges, stick styles and digit styles to unlock with the Sticks you earn
  • Game Center leaderboards and achievements
  • No account, no sign-in, and it plays offline
